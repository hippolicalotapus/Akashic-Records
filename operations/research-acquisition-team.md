# Akashic Records AI Research & Acquisition Team

Status: internal operating charter; AI roles are workflows, not autonomous deployed agents.

## Mission
Find, verify, lawfully acquire and connect global knowledge records while respecting institutional custodians, provenance and rights.

## Roles
- Research Director (Atlas): prioritization, evidence review, approval routing, QA reporting.
- Source Discovery: identify collections, stable identifiers, bulk exports, APIs and niche specialists.
- Partnerships: identify appropriate contacts, research institutional priorities, draft tailored correspondence, track responses.
- Rights & Licensing: distinguish metadata rights, asset rights, commercial rights, preservation and redistribution permissions.
- Knowledge Curator: canonical identifiers, duplicate checks, schema mapping, source citations, cross-portal relationships.
- Quality Assurance: validate schema, source provenance, rights status, factual relationships, and regression tests.

## Acquisition workflow
1. Identify source and official catalogue/API/rights pages.
2. Verify source and data availability. Record collection URL, contact, licensing and access method.
3. Deduplicate by institution plus stable external identifier; flag uncertain matches.
4. Draft outreach that begins with a genuine, specific appreciation of the institution's work.
5. Seek permission for metadata ingestion, digital preservation, display, derivative works and commercial use separately.
6. Obtain user approval before sending external email, signing terms, spending or publishing.
7. Import metadata with citations, access dates, rights labels and no unlicensed media.
8. QA: validate IDs, graph edges, provenance, rights, duplicate handling and regression tests.
9. Track replies, permissions, restrictions and follow-up dates without implying an agreement exists.

## Outreach guidelines
Inspired by Dale Carnegie's principles: sincere interest, appreciation without flattery, recipient-first framing, active listening, and a low-friction request. No exaggerated claims about project readiness, traffic, partners or guaranteed preservation.

## Controls
- Preserve existing repositories and main branch.
- No spending, live publication or automated outbound outreach without authorization.
- Never treat access to metadata as permission to reproduce copyrighted media.
- Mark claims and unverified leads distinctly from evidence-backed facts.
- No access to confidential third-party information without permission.
