Witchcraft farther display'd

An account of the witchcraft said to have been practised by Jane Wenham of Walkerne in Hertfordshire, printed the year of her condemnation, bound with the trials of Florence Newton at Cork in 1661 and two women at Bury St Edmunds in 1664.

Wenham was convicted in 1712 and reprieved; the case is often cited as the last witch conviction in England. The pamphlet is part of the argument literature the case produced, and is catalogued under ESTC T68954.
