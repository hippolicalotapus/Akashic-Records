Passing events, or the tail of the comet of 1853

George Cruikshank draws the comet as a figure with a human head, its tail made of the year's events strung out behind it. Designed and etched by Cruikshank as a year-end satire.

Cruikshank was the century's most prolific caricaturist and, by the 1850s, a committed temperance campaigner — the tail's cargo of scandals and causes reflects both.
