Akashic Records

An index of records. Entries are catalogued, dated where the date is known, and marked where it is not. Nothing is presented as confirmed until a source is attached to it.

Scope

Records arrive from archives, institutions, and private holders. Each carries provenance. Where provenance is thin, the record says so rather than guessing.

Structure

  • Records are grouped by collection, not by chronology.
  • Every entry names its source or is flagged unsourced.
  • Entries are never deleted, only superseded.

Status

The catalogue is being built. The interface exists; the records are still going in. No counts, coverage claims, or dates are published until they can be checked.
