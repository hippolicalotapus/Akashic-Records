About

Everything written down is one server migration, one bankruptcy, or one quiet deletion away from being gone. Not lost to war or fire — just unlinked, and then a 404.

This archive exists so that when a page dies, the record of it does not. It sits behind Orbis Arcanorum, World Wonder Web, and World Wicked Web as their shared store of records.

How it works

  1. One record at a time. Each entry is catalogued by hand, with its source and an assessment of what may be shown.
  2. Copy first, publish second. The copy is made and hashed before anything appears. A record that cannot be produced from a copy is a claim, not a record.
  3. Rights decide what you see. Public domain material is shown whole. Copyrighted material is shown as metadata, excerpt, and a link, with the copy held dark behind it.
  4. Nothing is deleted. Corrections are new revisions. Entries are superseded, never removed. A takedown hides a record; it does not erase it.
  5. Dead links are the point. Sources are checked on a schedule. When one dies, the record is what remains.

What is not claimed

No coverage figures, no counts, no dates, until they can be checked. Where provenance is thin, the record says so rather than guessing. Where two sources disagree, both are kept.

How it is paid for

Memberships on the three portals, institutional seats, and grants. The records themselves stay free to read, because an archive behind a paywall is a filing cabinet with a lock on it.

Corrections and takedowns

If a record is wrong, it gets superseded. If a record is yours and you want it down, it is hidden and the copy is retained unless you ask for deletion.
